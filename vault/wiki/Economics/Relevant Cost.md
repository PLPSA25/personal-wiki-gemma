# Relevant Cost

Relevant cost focuses only on the additional costs that will be incurred as a result of a specific decision. It involves comparing the marginal costs of the various options over the expected life of the decision. It is important to consider the time horizon when measuring these costs.

## Key points

- The right comparison was between the marginal costs of the two options over the application’s expected life. ([[raw/Relevant Cost.docx|Relevant Cost]])
- Internal marginal costs included additional developers, licenses, infrastructure, updates, and support. ([[raw/Relevant Cost.docx|Relevant Cost]])
- Existing employee salaries or infrastructure costs should not automatically be included when spare capacity existed and those costs would be incurred regardless. ([[raw/Relevant Cost.docx|Relevant Cost]])
- Outsourcing costs included the vendor’s “build-and-run” fees, integration, and vendor management. ([[raw/Relevant Cost.docx|Relevant Cost]])
- Accurately measuring internal capacity was difficult. ([[raw/Relevant Cost.docx|Relevant Cost]])
- The right measure of cost depends on the horizon of a decision. ([[raw/Relevant Cost.docx|Relevant Cost]])
