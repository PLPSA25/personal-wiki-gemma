# AuraTech Case

The intercept of the fitted line does not have a meaningful real-world interpretation because an AI value of zero is outside the observed range of the data. The slope indicates that a one-unit increase in the AI estimate is associated with a change in the hammer price, but this association is not a direct causal effect. The model's results suggest that the AI estimate is informative but should not be taken as a direct valuation.
